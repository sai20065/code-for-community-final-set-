Model size
Gemma 4 E4B (≈4B effective parameters) for low-cost serving; 26B A4B MoE (≈3.8B active) as the high-accuracy tier.
Why fine-tune?
General LLMs do not know Karnataka's allocation of business: who fixes a pothole on a PWD road vs a GBA road.
Human in the loop
Officers can reassign; every correction becomes new training data. Full audit log of each decision.
Open weights
Gemma is open-weight: the State can host, audit and own the model — no vendor lock-in.
