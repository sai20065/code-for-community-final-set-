Business Model Canvas

Free for citizens · sustainable through institutional licences and public-programme funding
Key Partners
• GBA & city corporations, ULBs
• District administrations (DC offices)
• Line departments: BESCOM, BWSSB, PWD, Health, Education
• Karnataka e-Governance / CeG
• Google Cloud (credits, Vertex AI)
• Civic-tech NGOs, RWAs, colleges (NSS)
Key Activities
• AI triage, routing & hotspot detection
• Boundary data upkeep (wards, taluks)
• Officer onboarding & verification
• Model fine-tuning & audits
Value Propositions
Citizens: report in their own language in 20 seconds, get a receipt, see it fixed.
Officials: a prioritised, de-duplicated queue for their own area, AI briefings, evidence for budgets.
Government: faster redress, transparent accountability, data-driven ward planning.
Relationships
• Live status tracker & badges for citizens
• Dedicated dashboards per office
• Public, anonymised dashboard builds trust

Cost Structure
Cloud & AI inference (≈₹0.5–1 per ticket, indicative) · model training (one-off, credits-eligible) · support & onboarding staff · security audits
Revenue & Sustainability
Per-ward / per-district SaaS licence for government · grants (Smart Cities, Digital India, CSR) · premium planning analytics for departments · always free for citizens
