How Prajadhwani works — from a citizen's voice to an official's action

End-to-end flow implemented in the current prototype (step 5 routing model is proposed)
1
Speak, type or snapOne screen: voice, text and photo in 10 Indian languages. No Aadhaar, phone or email needed.
➜
2
Instant receiptA ticket ID (e.g. PD-…-PRB-YYMMDD-0001) is issued the moment it is saved — even if AI is slow.
➜
3
AI understandsTranscribe, translate to English, caption the photo, classify the theme and score priority.
➜
4
GPS → every layerPoint-in-polygon resolves ward (BBMP + GBA), assembly seat, taluk, district and Lok Sabha seat.
➜
5
Route & groupSimilar reports form hotspots. Proposed Gemma 4 router names the responsible officer (DC fallback).
➜
6
Act & close the loopOfficials see it on their dashboard, update status; the citizen tracks Filed → Resolved live.
